About

The Band

This is Junonuno. An other-worldly artistic group built by DJ Juno and Nuno. The (sometimes) Bristol-based duo present catchy, old-school dance songs with a 90s house-inspired soul and pop-adjacent lyrics.

You'll find DJ Juno on the guitar — he's also the tech whizz behind those beats — while Nuno is the ethereal voice leading you to boogie land. On stage, they're joined by Uno on bass and Twono banging the drums, who create the unforgettable atmosphere at a Junonuno gig. You really have to see it to believe it.

Fun fact: Junonuno love to do human things like sit in a chair, eat food and play sports.
⚠ Warning: Do NOT try their moves at home if you live on a planet with gravity.
