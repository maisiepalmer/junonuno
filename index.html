The Band

An other-worldly artistic group built by DJ Juno and Nuno.

The (sometimes) Bristol-based duo present catchy, old-school dance songs with a 90s house-inspired soul and pop-adjacent lyrics. DJ Juno handles guitar and the tech wizardry behind the beats. Nuno is the ethereal voice leading you to boogie land.
